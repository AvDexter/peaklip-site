Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Peaklip is video editing software. It works on videos you give it: it finds the strongest moments, cuts them into vertical clips, frames the webcam and gameplay, and adds captions from what's said in the video. It does not generate images, video, faces or voices, and it cannot make anyone appear to say or do something they didn't. This policy is part of our Terms of Service.

Use videos you have the right to use

  • Only clip and post videos you made, or that you have the owner's permission to clip and post.
  • Follow the rules of every platform you post to (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and others).

Not allowed

Don't use Peaklip to make, edit or post:

  • Sexual content involving minors — ever. We report it to the authorities.
  • Sexually explicit or pornographic content.
  • Content that harasses, threatens, bullies or doxxes anyone, or that promotes violence, terrorism or self-harm.
  • Hate speech or content that attacks people for who they are.
  • Misleading edits meant to deceive — for example cutting someone's words so they appear to say something they didn't — impersonation, scams or spam.
  • Content that infringes copyright, trademarks or anyone's privacy, including other people's videos you don't have permission to use.
  • Anything illegal where you live or where you post it.

Don't misuse the service

  • Don't share accounts, resell access, or create accounts to get extra free trials or credits.
  • Don't attack, overload, scrape or reverse-engineer Peaklip's servers or try to get around plans, credits or limits.

What happens if this policy is broken

We may warn you, suspend or close your account, and report illegal content to the authorities. You stay responsible for everything you publish. To report misuse, email hello@peaklip.com.

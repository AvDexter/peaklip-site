What Peaklip is

Peaklip is a Windows app that finds the best moments in your videos and turns them into short clips with captions, ready for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok. It is video editing software: it works only on videos you give it and does not generate images, video, faces or voices. Transcription, moment detection and editing run on your own PC. Nothing is posted without your approval.

Peaklip's Scan shows a YouTube channel's public video statistics, using YouTube API Services, and links to the videos on YouTube. It doesn't download anything. By using Scan you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies to that data.

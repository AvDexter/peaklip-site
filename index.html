Questions

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet — Peaklip is a Windows 10 and 11 app for now.

Do I need a powerful PC?

It runs on most modern Windows PCs. An NVIDIA graphics card makes transcription and rendering much faster, but it isn't required.

Can I clip anyone's videos?

Only videos you own or have the creator's permission to clip and post. Peaklip asks you to confirm that before it imports anything from YouTube.

Does it post automatically?

No. You approve every clip and its text first. YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels can be posted from the app; for TikTok, Peaklip gets everything ready and you post it with one paste.

What does a credit buy?

One clip. A 15-, 30- or 60-second clip each costs one credit when it's made.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere — they stay on your PC. Your account only stores your name, email and sign-in details.

How do I install it?

Download the installer and run it. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the installer isn't code-signed yet — click “More info”, then “Run anyway”. Peaklip needs 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

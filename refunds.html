Refund policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Try first, then decide

Every plan starts with a 3-day free trial with 10 credits, so you can see what Peaklip does with your own videos before paying anything. Cancel before the trial ends and you're not charged.

No refunds after payment

Because you can try Peaklip for free first, payments for a subscription are not refundable, including partial months and unused credits.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel whenever you like in the app: Settings → Plan & credits → Manage subscription. Your plan and credits stay until the end of the month you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Exceptions

  • If you were charged by mistake (for example twice for the same month), we'll refund the extra charge.
  • If we close your account without you breaking our terms, we refund the unused, already-paid part of your subscription.
  • This policy doesn't take away any refund rights you have under the law where you live.

How refunds are paid

Payments are handled by Creem (creem.io), our reseller and Merchant of Record, which processes any refund to your original payment method. To ask about a charge, email hello@peaklip.com or see Creem's help for customers.

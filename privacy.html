Privacy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Peaklip is a Windows app that turns your videos into short clips. It's built so that your videos never leave your PC unless you choose to post a clip. This page explains exactly what we store and why.

The short version

  • Your videos and clips stay on your PC. Transcription, moment detection and editing run locally. We never receive your footage.
  • Smart picks (on by default, can be switched off) sends a few small still frames and the words said in each candidate moment to be judged by an AI model. Never the video, and nothing is kept.
  • Your Peaklip account stores your name, email address and sign-in details on our server.
  • Your YouTube and Instagram connections are stored only on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager. We never see them.
  • Scan looks up public YouTube video statistics through YouTube API Services; it doesn't use your YouTube account.
  • This website sets no cookies and loads no trackers unless you click “Accept” on its cookie banner. Then Meta’s Pixel measures which of our ads brought you here (details). Your account page never uses them.

What our server stores

When you create an account and sign in, our server keeps:

  • your name and email address;
  • when you confirmed you're 18 or older and accepted the Terms, and which version of the Terms it was;
  • your plan, credits and credit history (for example “clip made” or “plan renewed”);
  • your password as a one-way scrypt hash — never the password itself;
  • email confirmation codes (hashed, valid for 15 minutes) and sign-in sessions (stored only as a hash);
  • a device identifier for the PC you sign in from — a one-way hash, not your Windows ID itself — used to keep sign-ins and free trials fair;
  • records of recent sign-in and code attempts (which can include your IP address), used only to block password guessing and cleared out over time;
  • bug reports you choose to send, with your email so we can follow up.

We use this only to run your account, keep it secure and answer your reports. We don't sell it, rent it or use it for advertising.

What stays on your PC

Your imported videos, transcripts, found moments, rendered clips, post text, settings and the logins for the platforms you connect are stored on your own computer. Peaklip's analysis (speech recognition and moment ranking) runs on your PC too.

Posting to YouTube and Instagram

When you connect YouTube or Instagram, you sign in on the platform's own page and Peaklip receives an access key that is saved in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. When you post a clip, it is uploaded directly from your PC to that platform. Peaklip only uses this access to upload the clips you approve and to show which account is connected — never to read, change or delete anything else. You can disconnect at any time in the app, and revoke access in your Google account or Meta settings.

Peaklip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

TikTok isn't connected at all: Peaklip prepares the clip and caption, and you post it yourself on TikTok.

Smart picks

To find the moments worth clipping, Peaklip first measures your video on your PC. With Smart picks on, it then sends, for each candidate moment (usually 20–30 per video): about six small still frames of the game (512 pixels wide), up to three small close-ups of your webcam, the transcript lines of that moment and two measured numbers. Our server passes them to Anthropic's Claude model, which says whether something happens and where to cut. Neither our server nor Peaklip keeps the frames or text afterwards; Anthropic processes them only to answer and doesn't use them to train its models. We keep a count of how many moments and videos you had judged (with a scrambled code for each video, so re-checking the same video the same day isn't counted twice — never its name or contents), to apply your plan's Smart picks scans and stop overuse. You can switch Smart picks off in Settings → Clips; Peaklip then picks moments on your PC only.

Scan and YouTube API Services

When you use Scan, the channel link or @handle you paste is sent to our server, which looks up that channel's public videos and their view, like and comment counts using YouTube API Services. Nothing is downloaded, and your YouTube account isn't used. The results are kept in the server's memory for up to an hour, so scanning the same channel again doesn't use YouTube's quota, and are then discarded; we don't save them to our database. We keep a short-lived count of how many scans you ran, to stop overuse. YouTube's own Terms of Service apply to Scan, and Google's Privacy Policy explains how Google handles data.

Your account page on this website

On your account page you sign in with your Peaklip email and password to see your credits and history, buy or cancel a plan, or delete your account. The page talks only to Peaklip's server. The sign-in key is kept in that browser tab's session storage and is gone when you close the tab or sign out — no cookies.

Cookies and ad measurement on this website

The home page shows a cookie banner. If you click Decline, or ignore it, nothing from Meta is loaded and no cookie is set. If you click Accept, the page loads the Meta Pixel: it sets Meta cookies and sends Meta the page you visited, whether you clicked “Download”, and technical details such as your browser, device and IP address. We use this only to see which of our ads lead to visits and downloads, and to show our ads to people more likely to be interested. We see totals, not you. Meta Platforms Ireland receives this data and may link it to a Meta account you're signed in to, as described in Meta's Privacy Policy.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage (not a cookie). Change it any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the home page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Who helps us run it

  • Railway hosts our account server and its database.
  • Creem sells the subscriptions as our reseller (merchant of record): it handles checkout, your card details, invoices and taxes. We receive your plan and payment status, never your card.
  • An email delivery service sends your confirmation and password-reset codes.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard web-server logs for security.
  • Google (YouTube API Services) answers Scan's look-ups of public video statistics.
  • Anthropic runs the AI model behind Smart picks; it receives the small frames and transcript lines described above.
  • Meta measures our ads on this website, only if you accept cookies (see above).

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Confirmation codes expire after 15 minutes, and sign-in attempt records are cleared out over time. When you delete your account (or ask us to), we delete your account data, credit history and bug reports, except anything we're legally required to keep. We keep a short note that the account was deleted (its email and when), and the anonymous device hashes that already used a free trial, so trials stay one per PC. Creem keeps its own payment records as the seller.

Your choices

You can delete your account yourself at any time, in the app (Settings → Plan & credits → Delete account) or on your account page. You can also ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by emailing hello@peaklip.com. We'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU, the UK or Türkiye, you also have the right to complain to your data-protection authority.

Children

Peaklip is only for people aged 18 or older. Everyone confirms this when creating an account, and we don't knowingly keep accounts for anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, tell us at hello@peaklip.com and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
